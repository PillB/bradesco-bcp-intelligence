# 21. NEGATIVE SEARCH

For every strong conclusion search for reasons it might be wrong.

Examples:

“in production”
→ search pilot, PoC, beta, limited, test.

“successful”
→ search closure, integration, decline, discontinued, replacement.

“X% improvement”
→ search baseline, measurement period, denominator, attribution.

“company leads competitor”
→ test size, country, regulatory and disclosure effects.

---

# 22. ALTERNATIVE HYPOTHESES

For important findings formulate competing explanations.

Example:

Observation:
Company A publicly discusses more emerging technologies.

Possible explanations:

- genuine greater maturity;
- larger R&D budget;
- greater company scale;
- different disclosure strategy;
- different regulation;
- more experimental activity but lower production conversion.

Search for evidence discriminating among them.
