Headlines

What this plan actually says

1 · Ursa is more distinctive than it looks

The bear, the gram, the Art Nouveau lean, the two-bar (espresso + coldbrew) layout, and named drinks like Ursagroni and Maracumango Coldbrew add up to a brand that already does what Fresh Cup (2025) says works in 2025-26: identity over trend-hopping. The plan refines this rather than replacing it.

2 · The biggest gap is distribution, not product

Ursa has a real roastery, real drinks, and a real atmosphere. What it lacks is review-channel presence (zero TripAdvisor reviews), a Google Business Profile that converts, a creator network, a hospitality-tourism pipeline into the 8+ hotels within walking distance, and — surfaced in the deep-dive research pass — a website. All 5 main Miraflores competitors (Punto, Neira, Bisetti, Puku Puku, Terrua) have one. The plan fixes this in the first 30 days.

3 · Hormozi + Sutherland work, with caveats

Hormozi's Value Equation and offer-stack discipline translate cleanly to a café (bundle, anchor, continuity). Sutherland's perceived-value lens is even more useful: low-cost psychological changes (story cards, named preparations, the bear as a recurring character) often beat expensive rational ones. The plan adopts both with explicit "do not" lists.

4 · Conservative refinement wins

Of three brand-evolution levels (conservative / distinctive / experimental), the plan recommends Level 1 as the permanent system, Level 2 as a 6-month growth skin, and Level 3 only as seasonal expression. Total rebrand is explicitly off the table.

5 · Every bet is testable cheaply

Eight experiments (EXP-01 through EXP-08) cover the riskiest assumptions. Most cost S/. 0–560 to run. Each has a success metric and a stop rule. If something does not work in 14–30 days, it is killed cleanly and documented, not doubled-down on.

6 · The 90-day plan is owned, not aspirational

72-hour / 30 / 60 / 90-day plans each name an owner, a dependency, a metric, and a stopping rule. Budget scenarios in PEN: lean (S/. 2,500 / month), moderate (S/. 7,200 / month), growth (S/. 16,500 / month). The owner picks one and the plan adjusts.

7 · Deep-dive · ownable space sits orthogonal to competitors

The second research pass surfaced 7 new verified evidence items (E-21 through E-27). Punto Café won Premios Somos 2024. Neira has 4+ locations including a WorkCafé cobrand. Bisetti owns "escuela de café". Puku Puku owns "microlotes". Terrua charges US$25 for a paid tasting. Ursa's ownable space — bear + two bars + named-portmanteau drinks + "un gramo a la vez" — sits orthogonal to all of these. See Module 02 §2.5 for the full picture.

8 · NEW · Ursa Mañana subscription works on marginal-cost math

A S/. 20/month unlimited-coffee subscription (Mon–Fri 7–10am, no milk drinks, in-store only, 1 cup/visit) is profitable at almost any reasonable usage pattern because Ursa's own-roastery keeps marginal cost at S/. 1.20–1.80/cup (verified benchmark: specialty range US$0.75–1.20). At default assumptions (3 visits/week, 60% side attach, S/. 4.50 side margin), net profit is S/. 35.60/subscriber/month. The real risk is cannibalization, not per-cup cost — addressed in Module 08 with an interactive calculator and EXP-11 pilot.

Open questions

One grouped clarification for the owner

Most missing business data (average ticket, repeat-visit rate, margin, customer database, campaign history) is handled by lean/moderate/growth scenarios throughout the plan. Six questions would materially improve the plan if answered, but none blocks the 72-hour launch. The owner should answer them in a single batched reply when convenient.

Operational data

  • Monthly marketing budget range (lean / moderate / growth)?
  • Average ticket size and best / worst sellers from POS?
  • Current repeat-visit rate or any loyalty data?

Audience & assets

  • Size of existing customer email / WhatsApp list with consent?
  • Staff capacity for classes, cuppings, or creator collaborations?
  • Owner-supplied logo and packaging asset pack for visual verification of the Art Nouveau / bear motif / palette?
