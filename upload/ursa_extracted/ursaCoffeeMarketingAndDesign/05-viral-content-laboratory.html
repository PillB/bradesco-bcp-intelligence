Module 05 · Viral Content Laboratory

Specific beats clever. The bear is the character; the gram is the rhythm.

This module contains 24 original content concepts, 10 complete scripts, 3 repeatable series, 3 creator briefs, 3 UGC mechanisms, and a four-week pilot calendar — all specific to Ursa, Miraflores, the bear, the two bars, and the verified menu. No generic "show behind the scenes" advice. Virality is not guaranteed. The system is designed to learn cheaply which content earns attention and which does not.

Content concepts · 24 Complete scripts · 10 Repeatable series · 3 Creator briefs · 3 UGC mechanisms · 3 Pilot calendar · 4 weeks

Reality check — read before producing anything

Virality is not guaranteed. A small café with 4,746 followers cannot engineer virality on demand, and any consultant who promises otherwise is selling noise. What this module does is design a system in which (a) every post is on-brand and on-message even if it never goes viral, (b) each post has a measurable signal so the team can learn what works, (c) the rare viral moment is amplified quickly, and (d) no time is wasted on generic content that could be any café in any city. The 2025-26 coffee-shop Instagram playbook (Fresh Cup, socialmon.ai) confirms that identity-led accounts outperform trend-hopping ones. This module is built around identity.

5.2

10 complete scripts (Reel format, 22–45 seconds)

Each script is fully written — hook, beats, on-screen text, voice/caption, CTA, hashtag set. All copy is in Spanish (Peru) per the working assumptions. Each script ends with the verified tagline "Un gramo a la vez" as the system-level sign-off.

Script 1 · Reel · 28s

Un gramo a la vez — V60 weighed to the gram

Hook (0–3s) · Close-up of a digital scale reading 0.0g. A hand places a V60 on top. Tares to 0.0g. Caption on screen: "¿Cuánto importa un gramo?"

Beat 1 (3–10s) · Hand pours ground coffee onto the filter. Scale climbs: 5.2g... 12.1g... 17.4g... 18.0g. Stops. Caption: "18.0g. Exactos."

Beat 2 (10–18s) · Kettle pour. Bloom. Slow spiral. Scale climbs: 50g... 150g... 288g. Caption: "1:16. Agua a 92°C."

Beat 3 (18–24s) · Final drip. Drop into cup. Steam rises. Barista lifts cup. Caption: "Un gramo a la vez."

End card (24–28s) · Bear silhouette watermark + Alcanfores 183 + tagline. CTA: "Pídela en barra."

Voice/caption · No voice-over. Ambient sound only (pour, drip, crackle of crust). Caption text on screen in Cormorant Garamond italic.

5.3

3 repeatable series

Series are the spine of content. Each series below is a repeating format with a fixed structure and a variable subject. Once a series is established, production speed doubles because the template is reused. Each series has a cadence, a target, and a kill rule.

Series A

Un Gramo a la Vez — weekly technique

Format · 22–35s Reel, weekly on Tuesday.

Structure · Same on-screen text style ("Un gramo a la vez"); same hook (scale at 0.0g); same end card (bear paw + Alcanfores 183). Variable: brew method (V60, Aeropress, Chemex, espresso, cold brew, etc.) and ratio.

Target · 8,000 cumulative accounts reached across first 4 episodes; ≥20 WhatsApp sign-ups.

Kill rule · If episode 2 reach is <1,200 accounts, redesign hook and retest with 2 more episodes before scaling.

See EXP-04 for full test design.

Series B

Dos Barras — paired drinks

Format · 30s Reel, bi-weekly on Friday.

Structure · Split-screen: espresso bar preparation on left, coldbrew bar preparation on right. Both drinks slid across the bar at the end. Variable: the specific drinks (e.g., flat white + coldbrew tonic; Ursagroni + Maracumango; etc.).

Target · 5,000 cumulative accounts reached across first 4 episodes; ≥15 first-visit bundle redemptions.

Kill rule · If 2 consecutive episodes <1,000 accounts reached, redesign split-screen format or rotate to single-bar series.

Series C

El Origen de la Semana — single origin spotlight

Format · 5-slide carousel + 35s Reel, weekly on Wednesday.

Structure · Slide 1: origin name + map. Slide 2: altitude, process, producer. Slide 3: roast date, roast curve. Slide 4: tasting notes. Slide 5: pairing recommendation + CTA. Reel: 35-second origin story with optional voice-over.

Target · ≥3 retail bean purchases per spotlight; ≥1 new WhatsApp sign-up per spotlight.

Kill rule · If 3 consecutive spotlights <2 retail purchases, redesign carousel or kill series.

3 creator briefs

Each brief is for a specific type of Lima-based creator. Briefs are written so a creator can say yes or no without a long call. All briefs include deliverable, scope, comp, exclusivity, content ownership, and brand rules.

Brief A · Lima food & coffee creator (10k–60k followers)

Deliverable · 1 Reel + 1 Story sequence featuring the two-bar tour and one named drink (Ursagroni or Maracumango).

Scope · 1.5-hour visit to Alcanfores 183 during off-peak (3pm–5pm weekday). Creator receives complimentary Doble Barra Ursa bundle + a 250g retail bean bag.

Comp · S/. 200 fee + comp items above. Total value ~S/. 280.

Exclusivity · No other Miraflores specialty-café content from this creator for 14 days before and after publication.

Content ownership · Creator owns content; Ursa receives perpetual license to repost with credit. Ursa may use stills in paid ads with separate negotiation.

Brand rules · Use "Ursa Coffee Roasters" (not "Ursa Café"); tagline "Un gramo a la vez" in caption; mention Alcanfores 183; no drinking from the bear mug joke; no claims of "best coffee in Lima".

5.5

3 UGC & customer-participation mechanisms

UGC (user-generated content) is the lowest-cost, highest-trust content. Three mechanisms, each with a clear trigger and reward. None require customers to download an app or fill a form.

UGC-1 · "#MiMañanaUrsa" photo wall

Trigger · Customer posts a photo of their Ursa morning (cup + pastry + storefront) with the hashtag #MiMañanaUrsa and tags @ursacoffeeperu.

Reward · Ursa reposts to Stories within 48 hours. Customer receives a free house-made cookie on their next visit (mention the repost at the bar).

Scale target · 8 UGC posts per month after 60 days.

Kill rule · If <3 posts/month after 60 days, redesign reward (e.g., free filter coffee instead of cookie).

UGC-2 · "Tráelo de viaje" bean souvenir program

Trigger · Customer buys a retail bean bag and posts a photo of it in a non-Lima location (Cusco, Arequipa, international) with #UrsaEnElMundo and tags @ursacoffeeperu.

Reward · Featured on a permanent "Ursa en el mundo" map wall in-store. Free cookie on next visit.

Scale target · 5 destination posts per month after 90 days.

Kill rule · If <2 posts/month after 90 days, redesign to in-Lima scope only.

UGC-3 · Saturday cupping notes wall

Trigger · Saturday cupping attendees write their tasting notes on a small card and pin to the cupping-notes wall.

Reward · Best note of the month (selected by barista) earns a free private cupping for the attendee + 1 friend.

Scale target · 80% of cupping attendees leave a note by week 4.

Kill rule · If <40% participation after 4 weeks, redesign card or stop program.

  • Reach of each post published that week (Reels > 3s plays, carousel swipes, Story completion)

5.7

Test & amplification method

The method below is the system's immune system. It ensures good content scales and bad content dies. It costs almost nothing to operate and runs weekly.

Test tier (every piece, always)

  • Baseline · Every post gets 24 hours of organic reach with no promotion.
  • Signal capture · Reach, plays > 3s, completion rate, saves, shares, comments, WhatsApp sign-ups attributed.
  • Friday review · 30-minute team review. Each piece classified: Amplify, Iterate, or Kill.

Amplify tier (only for pieces that earn it)

  • Threshold · Reach >2× the account's 30-day median, OR saves >50, OR WhatsApp sign-ups >5.
  • Action · Boost post with S/. 100–200 paid budget, 1km Miraflores radius, 7-day flight.
  • Re-share to Stories with a "Gracias por tanto cariño" frame.
  • Pin to profile for 14 days.
  • Document in a "winners" file for future iteration.

Iterate tier

  • Threshold · Reach between 0.5× and 2× the median; some signals but not breakout.
  • Action · Identify one variable to change (hook, thumbnail, opening 3 seconds, caption, CTA). Retest within 14 days.
  • Rule · Iterate once. If the second variant does not cross the Amplify threshold, kill the format.

Kill tier

  • Threshold · Reach <0.5× median; <10 saves; zero attributed WhatsApp sign-ups.
  • Action · Do not repeat format. Document the failure mode in the "winners" file as a negative example.
  • Rule · Never double down on a killed format. Move on.

What this method does not do

  • Does not chase trends. Identity-led content outperforms trend-hopping for coffee shops in 2025-26 (Fresh Cup, socialmon.ai).
  • Does not pay for fake engagement. Ever.
  • Does not boost posts that performed poorly organically. Paid amplification rewards winners, not rescues losers.
  • Does not optimize for follower count. Followers are a vanity metric; reach, saves, shares, and WhatsApp sign-ups are signal metrics.
  • Does not promise virality. The system is built to learn cheaply, not to manufacture hits.
5.8

Skeptical revision pass

Skeptic 1

"You can't know if these scripts will go viral."

Correct — and the module says so explicitly in the Reality Check at the top. The scripts are designed to be on-brand and measurable even if they never go viral. The Test & Amplification method in §5.7 makes the system learn cheaply: most posts will not break out, and that is fine. The cost of a "failed" Reel is one staff-hour.

Skeptic 2

"24 concepts and 10 scripts in 4 weeks is too much for a small team."

The pilot calendar (§5.6) schedules 12 pieces of content across 4 weeks, not 24+10. The 24 concepts and 10 scripts are the pool from which the pilot and the 90-day plan draw. Concepts are reusable: each "Un Gramo a la Vez" episode is one concept reproduced with different brew methods. The system is designed for a small team with limited production capacity.

Skeptic 3

"Creator briefs at S/. 150–300 won't attract quality creators."

True for top-tier Lima creators (50k+ followers), who are out of scope for a single café. The briefs target mid-tier creators (3k–60k followers) for whom S/. 150–300 plus comp and an interesting experience is fair value, especially with the educational angle of Brief C. Brief C in particular should attract educators who value the roaster-access angle over fee.
