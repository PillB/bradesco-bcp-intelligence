A.4

Open questions for the owner (grouped)

Six questions would materially improve the plan if answered. None blocks the 72-hour launch (§7.1). The owner should answer them in a single batched reply when convenient.

Operational data

  1. Q1. Monthly marketing budget range (lean / moderate / growth)?
  2. Q2. Average ticket size and best / worst sellers from POS?
  3. Q3. Current repeat-visit rate or any loyalty data?

Audience & assets

  1. Q4. Size of existing customer email / WhatsApp list with consent?
  2. Q5. Staff capacity for classes, cuppings, or creator collaborations?
  3. Q6. Owner-supplied logo and packaging asset pack for visual verification of the Art Nouveau / bear motif / palette?

Why these six and only these six

Other missing business data (gross margin, peak-hour capacity, campaign history, ingredient costs) is handled by the lean/moderate/growth scenario structure throughout the dossier. These six questions are different: their answers materially change specific workstreams. Q1 changes which budget scenario the 30-day plan runs. Q2 and Q3 calibrate the metrics in §4.9. Q4 seeds the WhatsApp Bean Drop list (EXP-06). Q5 calibrates the cupping program (EXP-03) and creator collaboration capacity. Q6 unblocks the Level-2 design system rollout (§7.3). Until they are answered, the plan runs as written with clearly labeled scenario ranges.

A.6

Conflicting-information log

The completion standard requires that conflicting information be identified. Two conflicts surfaced during research; both are documented below.

Conflict 1 · Opening hours

Instagram bio says Lun–Sab 7:30am–9:00pm, Dom 8:30am–8:00pm. Rappi listing shows 08:00–20:30 delivery window. Corner.inc shows Monday 7:30 AM–9:00 PM.

Resolution · The dossier uses the Instagram bio hours as authoritative (7:30am opening, 9:00pm closing Mon–Sat). The Rappi delivery hours are shorter than in-store hours, which the dossier flags as a fixable gap (§2.3, §4.6) — delivery hours should be aligned with in-store hours wherever Rappi allows.

Conflict 2 · "URSA" brand identity

Web search returned results for an unrelated "URSA" roastery/gallery in Bridgeport, CT, USA (ursabpt.com) with Instagram posts about "URSA Café" and "URSA Gallery". This is not the Peruvian brand.

Resolution · The dossier explicitly excludes all Bridgeport URSA references. Only @ursacoffeeperu and the Alcanfores 183 location are treated as authoritative. The disambiguation is noted in §1.1 of the index, in §2.1, and in this appendix.
