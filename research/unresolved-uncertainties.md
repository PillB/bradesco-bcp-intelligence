# Unresolved Uncertainties

**Last updated:** 2026-08-01 (after Macrocycle 10)

## Owner data required (does not block 72-hour launch)

1. **Monthly marketing budget range** — lean/moderate/growth scenarios absorb this gap
2. **Average ticket size and best/worst sellers from POS** — calculator uses benchmark ranges, not invented point estimates
3. **Current repeat-visit rate or loyalty data** — the itsloyaleats wallet card can provide this once integrated
4. **Size of existing customer email/WhatsApp list with consent** — lean scenario assumes zero
5. **Staff capacity for classes, cuppings, creator collaborations** — cupping night is EXP-06 with a stop rule
6. **Owner-supplied logo and packaging asset pack** — palette tokens are labeled "approximate" until owner-verified

## Evidence gaps

1. **Ursa-specific customer reviews** — TripAdvisor shows ~0 reviews; customer-voice themes are illustrative (inferred from competitor patterns), not from a coded Ursa review sample. A proper study requires ≥50 Ursa-specific reviews or a consented survey.
2. **Official brand guidelines** — the palette, typography, and bear motif are observed/proposed, not official. Provenance labels now distinguish these.
3. **Phone number conflict** — mindtrip.ai lists +51 938 636 645; Instagram lists +51 973 619 428. Unresolved.

## Framework limitations

1. **Hormozi Value Equation** — suggestive, not proven in café contexts. Labeled as such.
2. **Sutherland perceived-value principles** — moderate, largely anecdotal. Each lever is a testable hypothesis.
3. **Endowed Progress Effect** — 82% lift figure applies to 10-stamp cards; 8-visit card effect is unvalidated. Labeled as indicative.
4. **Ehrenberg-Bass** — strong science but tested primarily in FMCG, not single-site cafés. Physical availability = local discoverability, not national distribution.

## Time-sensitive facts that will become stale

1. **Social statistics** (4,746 followers, 206 posts) — hardcoded from 2026-08-01 snapshot. Should be refreshed quarterly.
2. **CAM Café 2025 results** — published Dec 10, 2025. Next competition (2026) may change rankings.
3. **Competitor positions** — Punto Café, Neira, Monótono may open/close locations. Re-verify quarterly.
